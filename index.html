The process

From brief to live video in five steps. You focus on what matters.

We've built a repeatable system for turning a brand's goal into a creator's paycheck — and real revenue for everyone involved.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    You tell us your product, audience, budget, and campaign goals. We ask the right questions. No deck, no lengthy brief form — just a focused 30-minute conversation.

  2. 02

    Creator matching

    We compile a shortlist of creators whose subscriber base, engagement rate, and content style align with your buyers — not just whoever is available. You review and approve.

  3. 03

    Outreach & negotiation

    We approach each creator directly, build the working relationship, and negotiate pricing, deliverable format, timeline, and usage rights. You don't touch a single back-and-forth email.

  4. 04

    Campaign execution

    Contracts, briefs, creative approvals, and publishing deadlines are all managed through Brokoby. If something needs fixing before launch, we catch it — not you.

  5. 05

    Reporting & repeat

    Once live, you get a clear performance report: views, estimated reach, click-through data, and creator attribution. We identify what's worth repeating and propose the follow-up.

FAQ

Questions we hear before the first call.

How does Brokoby make money? +

We take a commission on each deal we broker — a percentage of the sponsorship value. There are no retainers, no platform fees, and no upfront charges. If a campaign doesn't happen, we don't charge. Our incentives are completely aligned with yours.

What size creators do you work with? +

Primarily YouTube creators in the 50K to 1M subscriber range — the segment where engagement rates are highest and deals are commercially meaningful without requiring enterprise budgets. The sweet spot for real ROI is usually mid-tier creators with genuine, active communities rather than passive mass audiences.

What categories do you cover? +

We work across YouTube broadly — tech, productivity, finance, software, lifestyle, education, and more. The common thread is that every creator we work with has an audience that makes purchasing decisions and trusts their recommendations. We match your brand to the creator community most likely to convert.

What does the sponsorship format look like? +

Typically 60–90 second mid-roll or end-roll integrations within an existing video, or dedicated videos where the entire piece revolves around your product. Dedicated videos command higher rates but deliver significantly more depth and conversion. We'll recommend the right format for your goals.

How do you verify that a creator's engagement is genuine? +

We review view-to-subscriber ratio, comment quality, upload consistency, and historical sponsored content performance. Channels that have inflated sub counts through incentivised subscriptions or bought engagement are easy to spot — and they don't make our shortlists. We look at channels the same way a serious media buyer would.

I'm a creator — how do I get into Brokoby's network? +

Send your channel and a brief note about your content to rafsan@brokoby.com. We review every application and prioritise creators whose audience aligns with the brand briefs we're actively working on. Once you're in the network, we bring deals to you — you don't have to pitch cold ever again.

How quickly can a campaign go live? +

From brief to live video, a standard campaign typically runs 2–4 weeks — depending on creator availability and creative approval cycles. Expedited timelines are possible for the right deal. We'll give you a realistic estimate on the discovery call.

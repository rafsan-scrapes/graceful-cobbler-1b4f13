Unless otherwise agreed in writing for a particular Campaign, the total sponsorship fee is payable in two installments: fifty percent (50%) of the total sponsorship fee is payable before the sponsored content is published; and the remaining fifty percent (50%) is payable fourteen (14) days after the sponsored content goes live. The Brand pays Brokoby the commission agreed for the Campaign and pays the remaining amount directly to the Creator. The applicable sponsorship fee, commission rate, payment amounts, payment schedule, and payment instructions for each Campaign will be confirmed in writing before the Campaign begins.
